I read through the project brief and thought of ways to explore the concepts I wrote about in my past entry. I'm always interested in the concept of surveillance and the way that it integrates our lives unscrupulously. Most of us are aware of it yet do very little to push back against it. WEEK 7: NOV 16th

This week's class was about reading and writing to files. This is helpful for my project because it is a journaling app that records users' behavior as they are journaling about their day. This will have to be a mix of NLP (natural language processor) and vanilla JS. This is not a project I am making in parallel with another class, but it may help with a research project that I am starting. I found the spacytextblob library, which will add sentiment analysis to the journal entries users make in the journal. This is an unexpected find that I am excited to explore.

WEEK 8: NOV 23rd

This week, we presented our projects to the class. My final application now has the spacytextblob library for sentiment analysis, as well as other spaCy features (like counting word categories). As previously mentioned, it also has Javascript features that count the amount of words, the time it took to write an entry, how many times the writer hesitated, and more. Some of the greatest challenges were defining a what counts as a hesitation/break when the user types and reading through the spaCy library to figure out what is useful to me vs what isn't. I got great feedback and ideas from my teammates. They really defined the two paths that this project could take: a therapy tool vs more of a commentary on the kinds of data that can be tracked about a person and the profile it can build on them. Some people seemed excited to try this tool on their own, so I would love to look into making it public. Some of my classmates have used Render to publish their websites, which I might look into as well.
