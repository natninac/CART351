WEEK 4: OCT 26th

This week's class was an introduction to Flask. We installed it & started learning the basics of data transfering. I read through the project brief and thought of ways to explore the concepts I wrote about in my past entry. I'm always interested in the concept of surveillance and the way that it integrates our lives unscrupulously. Most of us are aware of it yet do very little to push back against it. I'm thinking of an application that captures uncomfortably precise information and transparently displays it back to the user, to create a sense of unease and being watched. I have also been thinking about information logging and how our own relationships with data are changing our behaviors, specifically when it comes to memory and cognitive well-being (example: taking a photo of something makes us less likely to remember it, yet we take more photos than ever). I've also been interested in journaling, physical note-taking and documenting I would be interested to explore that in the context of this project, and developing methods of documenting that are more exploratory/useful than what is available out there.
